RECYCLE RIGHT

Recycling Guide

Know your waste and dispose of it responsibly.

📄

Paper

Newspapers, notebooks and cardboard.

Put in paper recycling bins.
🥤

Plastic

Clean plastic bottles and containers.

Check local recycling rules.
🍾

Glass

Glass bottles and jars.

Separate glass carefully.
🥫

Metal

Aluminium cans and metal containers.

Keep them clean before recycling.
🍂

Organic Waste

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps and leaves.

Use composting where possible.
🔋

E-Waste

Old batteries, chargers and electronics.

Use authorised e-waste collection centres.
